# Raw research — AI design tells, mid-2026 state

> Agente de investigación Fable, 2026-07-04. Fuentes web en vivo. Insumo para `docs/research/2026-07-04-fable-evolution.md`.

**Key development since April:** Adrian Krebs (Kadoa CEO) published a quantitative analysis (April 20, 2026) scoring 1,590 Show HN landing pages against 16 deterministic DOM/CSS "slop patterns" via Playwright — 22% heavy slop (4+ patterns), 32% mild, 46% clean. #1 on HN (333 points, 235 comments); its pattern list is the de-facto industry checklist, spawning detector tools (detectvibecode.com, VibeCheck Chrome extension, vibedetect.io) and a 50-rule catalog at impeccable.style/slop. **The tells are now machine-checkable — Eros's output can and will be scored by these exact heuristics.**

## (a) Current AI tells by category

### Typography
- **Inter as default, especially centered hero headlines** — Krebs pattern #1 ([adriankrebs.ch/blog/design-slop](https://www.adriankrebs.ch/blog/design-slop/)); "the default font in nearly every AI design tool" ([925studios.co](https://www.925studios.co/blog/ai-slop-web-design-guide), Mar 2026); part of the "shadcn fingerprint" ([freedesignmd.com](https://freedesignmd.com/blog/shadcn-looks-generic), Apr 28, 2026).
- **The LLM-favored combo: Space Grotesk + Instrument Serif + Geist** — named explicitly by Krebs and [impeccable.style/slop](https://impeccable.style/slop/). [vibecodekit.dev](https://vibecodekit.dev/ai-slop-design) adds **Poppins**.
- **The serif-italic accent word** — one italicized (often Instrument Serif) word inside an otherwise-Inter hero headline — Krebs pattern #3; "italic flourishes unearned by content" ([solodesign.cc](https://solodesign.cc/blog/ai-design-slop-the-tells/), Jun 6, 2026). Arguably THE 2026-H1 signature tell.
- **Instrument Serif now derided as lazy** ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/design/fonts-typography/serif-fonts-are-back-in-fashion-and-i-couldnt-be-happier)); serifs-in-general are the AI-brand cliché ([Creative Bloq: why AI brands are obsessed with serif fonts](https://www.creativebloq.com/design/fonts-typography/why-ai-brands-are-obsessed-with-serif-fonts)).
- **Second-wave alternatives are contaminated**: the viral "Inter is overused, try these" lists (Geist, Satoshi, General Sans, Rethink Sans, DM Sans, Host Grotesk, Figtree, Onest, Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit) circulated so widely they became the new rotation. Bricolage Grotesque now marketed against as "ubiquitous" ([TypeType](https://typetype.org/fonts/bricolage-grotesque-similar-fonts/)).
- **What reads human in 2026**: foundry faces with history/quirk — GT America, Söhne, Graphik, Aeonik, Neue Montreal, GT Alpina, Canela, Domaine, Tiempos, Editorial New ([Creative Boom 50 fonts](https://www.creativeboom.com/resources/top-50-fonts-in-2026/)); [WhatFontIs](https://www.whatfontis.com/blog/designers-are-punishing-ai-fonts-in-2026-and-its-making-type-more-human/): designers "punishing" soft geometric sans that "look like they came out of a prompt".
- Other tells: flat type hierarchy, all-caps body/labels, decorative monospace for body copy, single font family for the whole page.

### Color & effects
- **"VibeCode Purple" / purple-to-blue gradients** — still #1 tell; 10.7% of scanned sites used purple CTAs (Krebs). Root cause: Tailwind `bg-indigo-500` defaults ([dev.to/alanwest](https://dev.to/alanwest/why-every-ai-built-website-looks-the-same-blame-tailwinds-indigo-500-3h2p)).
- **The escape hatches are now tells too**: "safe emerald green when indigo is restricted"; the **amber-and-cream "warm accent" palette is "the em-dash of design"** (solodesign.cc); "warm cream or beige page background has become the default 'tasteful' AI surface" (impeccable.style). **Key H2-2026 shift: warm-minimal-cream is the NEW purple.**
- **Permanent dark mode** with medium-grey low-contrast body text + all-caps labels (Krebs #5–6); "neon-on-dark (cyan/violet) with glowing borders" (vibecodekit.dev); "dark backgrounds with colored box-shadow glows" (impeccable.style).
- **Glassmorphism** — one of Krebs's two dominant CSS fingerprints; #3 on Creative Boom's "trends creatives are so over in 2026" (Liquid Glass backlash). Only defensible as functional accent à la Linear ([studiomeyer.io](https://studiomeyer.io/en/blog/webdesign-trends-2026-reality-check)).
- Gradient orbs behind heroes, gradient text, gradients on stat numbers, colored glows behind cards/buttons, shadcn slate/zinc greys.

### Layout & composition
- **The full skeleton**: centered hero → badge/pill above H1 → 3 icon-top feature cards → logo cloud/stat banner → pricing (middle plan elevated) → FAQ accordion → CTA → footer ([dev.to/alanwest fix guide](https://dev.to/alanwest/how-to-fix-the-ai-generated-look-in-your-frontend-1ahh), May 18, 2026).
- **Colored left/top border strip on a card** — "almost as reliable a sign of AI-generated design as em-dashes" (Krebs); "the most recognizable tell of AI-generated UIs" (impeccable.style).
- **Badge/eyebrow pill above the H1** — Krebs #9; "uppercase eyebrow with decorative dot prefix" (solodesign.cc).
- **Icon tile stacked above heading = "the universal AI feature-card template"**; identical same-sized card grids; nested cards ("cardocalypse"); numbered 1·2·3 step rows; "01 / 02 / 03" section markers; horizontal stat banners; monotonous spacing.
- **Bento grids** — #9 on Creative Boom's over-it list; "default bento grids" named slop (vibecodekit.dev).
- **shadcn/ui untouched defaults** — the other dominant CSS fingerprint (Krebs). Fingerprint = slate/zinc + 8px radius everywhere + Inter + 1px slate-200 card borders (freedesignmd.com).

### Motion
- **Identical fade-in/fade-up applied uniformly**; "thirteen staggered entrance animations on page load" as a caught tell (solodesign.cc).
- **Missing/non-functional hover states; buttons that snap instead of easing** (925studios.co, vibecodekit.dev).
- **Bounce/elastic easing on UI, animating layout properties, scale-on-hover images** flagged as slop (impeccable.style).
- **"Motion for its own sake"** — #10 on Creative Boom's over-it list.
- Missing prefers-reduced-motion fallbacks.

### Iconography & imagery
- Centered oversized **Lucide icons** in feature cards; emoji as sidebar/nav icons (Krebs #14); floating 3D plastic blobs; "a blob in the header" ([Malewicz, Vibe Coding is OVER](https://michalmalewicz.medium.com/vibe-coding-is-over-5a84da799e0d), Mar 24, 2026); stock "diverse team at laptops" photos.

### Copy
- Em-dash overuse; "streamline/empower/supercharge/world-class/enterprise-grade"; vague headlines ("Build the future of work"); hedging; aphoristic section-ending cadence.

## (b) Verdict table for the April-2026 CANDIDATE rules

| April rule | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| "DM Sans, Space Grotesk, Outfit, Sora, Plus Jakarta Sans emerging AI fingerprint fonts" | **PARTIALLY SUPERSEDED — update list** | Space Grotesk confirmed top-tier tell. Canonical 2026 fingerprint set: **Inter, Geist, Space Grotesk, Instrument Serif** (+ Poppins). DM Sans/Outfit/Sora/Plus Jakarta second-tier (contaminated via viral alternative-lists). **Add: Geist, Instrument Serif, Satoshi, General Sans, Bricolage Grotesque, Figtree, Onest.** Nothing free is safe by name — safety comes from foundry faces or genuinely obscure historically-grounded choices. |
| "Generic glassmorphism = AI fingerprint 2026" | **STILL VALID — quantitatively codified** | One of Krebs's two dominant CSS fingerprints; #3 on over-it list. Narrow exception: functional glass à la Linear. |
| "Fade-up as only entry animation = AI tell" | **STILL VALID — broadened** | Uniform stagger cascades, snap transitions, missing hover states, decorative motion overall are all tells now. |
| "Uniform border-radius = template feel" | **STILL VALID with nuance** | The tell is the *untouched default value* (8/16px, rounded-2xl) + default-shadow pairing, not consistency per se. Deliberate extremes (0px, one signature value, 999px pill) read as intentional. |

## (c) Proposed anti-AI rules for 2026-H2 (testable)

**Typography**
1. No typeface from the fingerprint set {Inter, Geist, Space Grotesk, Instrument Serif, Poppins, Satoshi, DM Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit, Sora, General Sans, Bricolage Grotesque, Figtree, Onest} in any `font-family`.
2. No single italicized serif accent word inside a sans-serif hero headline.
3. ≥2 font families with display/body size ratio ≥ 3:1 above the fold (no flat hierarchy).
4. No uppercase eyebrow/kicker label (<14px, letter-spaced) directly above an H1 — zero "badge above H1" pills.
5. No monospace on body copy; mono only for actual code/data.

**Color & effects**
6. No purple/indigo/violet (hue 250–290) as primary accent; no purple→blue gradient anywhere.
7. No cream/beige "warm tasteful" surface + amber accent; no fallback "safe emerald on near-black" — palette must contain ≥1 color choice not predictable from the brief's industry.
8. No `backdrop-filter: blur()` on decorative cards; glass only over moving/functional content.
9. No colored box-shadow glow behind cards/buttons/hero art; no gradient orbs/blobs as hero backgrounds; no gradient-filled text or stat numbers.
10. Dark themes must pass WCAG AA (≥4.5:1) on body text.

**Layout**
11. No card with colored left/top border strip (≥2px accent).
12. No feature grid of 3+ identical cards with icon tile above heading; no exactly-three-cards-per-row section.
13. Section sequence must NOT match hero→features→logos/stats→pricing→FAQ→CTA; ≥1 section must break vertical centered stacking (asymmetric grid, bleed, or overlap).
14. No numbered 1·2·3 step rows, no "01/02/03" section markers, no horizontal stat banners.
15. Border-radius must be a deliberate vocabulary — never untouched 8px/16px/rounded-2xl defaults, never paired with default `shadow-lg p-6`.
16. ≥3 distinct section padding values per page (spacing rhythm).

**Motion**
17. ≥3 distinct reveal treatments per page (mask/clip, char/line splits, scale, draw-on…); fade-up on at most one section type; ≤4 total entrance animations on initial load.
18. Every interactive element has a designed hover/focus state with easing (no snaps, no bounce/elastic on UI, no scale-on-hover-image as the only idea); prefers-reduced-motion respected.

**Iconography & copy**
19. No default Lucide/Heroicons glyphs in rounded tint tiles; no emoji as nav icons; no 3D plastic blob illustrations.
20. Copy: zero of {"seamless","streamline","empower","supercharge","effortless","world-class","all-in-one","build the future","scale without limits"}; ≤1 em-dash per 300 words; every headline names something concrete.

**Meta-rule:** these patterns are scanned deterministically by public tools (detectvibecode.com, VibeCheck, vibedetect.io, impeccable.style, Krebs's Playwright script). **Eros should target 0–1 patterns on Krebs's 16-point checklist as a hard QA gate.**
